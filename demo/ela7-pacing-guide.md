# Grade 7 ELA — Fall Pacing Guide 2026

This is a fictional pacing guide for demonstration purposes.

## Unit 2: Argument Writing (Sept 28 – Oct 30)

Students read paired articles on whether schools should start later and write an argumentative essay with a claim, counterclaim, and at least two pieces of cited evidence.

- Standards: W.7.1 (argument writing), RI.7.8 (evaluate an argument).
- Key assignment: argumentative essay, final draft due **October 23, 2026**.
- Unit 2 test (reading an argument, identifying claims and evidence): **October 28, 2026**.
- Reassessment window: through November 11, 2026.

## Unit 3: Short Fiction and Theme (Nov 2 – Dec 11)

Students read four short stories and analyze how authors develop theme through character and conflict.

- Standards: RL.7.2 (theme), RL.7.3 (story elements interact).
- Key assignment: literary analysis paragraph, due **November 20, 2026**.
- Unit 3 test: **December 9, 2026**.

## Independent reading

Students read a self-selected book for 20 minutes, three days a week, and complete a monthly book talk. Leveled classroom library bins are organized by reading level.

## Rubric: argumentative essay (4-point scale)

- **Claim (4):** clear, arguable claim that directly answers the prompt.
- **Evidence (4):** at least two relevant, accurately cited pieces of evidence, each explained.
- **Counterclaim (4):** names a counterclaim and rebuts it with reasoning or evidence.
- **Organization (4):** logical paragraphs with transitions; conclusion restates the claim.
- **Conventions (4):** few errors that do not interfere with meaning.
