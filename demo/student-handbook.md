# Maple Grove Middle School — Student & Family Handbook 2026–27

This is a fictional handbook for demonstration purposes.

## Attendance

Students are expected to attend every school day. Families must report an absence by calling the attendance line before 9:00 a.m. Three unexcused tardies in a quarter result in a lunch detention. After five absences in a semester, the counselor contacts the family to build an attendance support plan.

## Make-up work

Students with an excused absence receive two school days for each day missed to turn in make-up work, without penalty. For long-term absences (five or more consecutive days) the case manager or counselor coordinates a make-up plan with each teacher.

## Late work

Late assignments are accepted until the end of the unit for up to 80% credit. Students with an IEP or 504 plan that lists "flexible deadlines" follow the timeline in their plan instead, with no penalty.

## Testing accommodations

Accommodations listed in a student's IEP or 504 plan apply to all classroom tests, quizzes, and state assessments. Extended time is usually 1.5x the standard time unless the plan states otherwise. Students who receive testing in a separate setting take tests in Room 114 (the Learning Center) and must be scheduled with the Learning Center at least two school days before the test.

## Grading

Grades are standards-based. Each standard is scored 1–4: 4 exceeds, 3 meets, 2 approaching, 1 beginning. Teachers post grades to the parent portal at least every two weeks. Students may reassess on any summative test once per unit within ten school days of the original test.

## Cell phones

Phones must be off and in lockers from the first bell to the last bell. A first violation is a warning; a second violation means the phone is held in the main office until the end of the day; a third requires a parent pickup.

## Parent communication

Teachers respond to family emails within two school days. Families who speak a language other than English can request a translated copy of any school letter or an interpreter for meetings through the main office. Progress reports are mailed home at the midpoint of each quarter.

## IEP and 504 meetings

Annual IEP reviews are scheduled by the case manager at least ten school days in advance. General education teachers are expected to submit a brief progress summary to the case manager no later than three school days before the meeting.

## Field trips

Permission slips must be returned five school days before a trip. Students with health plans must have their medication and a trained adult on every trip.
