# Local School: a guide for teachers

Local School answers questions about **your school's documents** and **your own class roster**. Every answer shows where it came from.

## 1. Open it

Open this link in Safari or Chrome:

**https://highlightcenter1234--localschool-app-web.modal.run**

- **Just looking?** Press **Try the demo**. You'll be a demo teacher at a fictional school (Maple Grove Middle School).
- **Have an account?** Sign in with the email and password your administrator gave you. To change your password, use the key icon at the bottom left.

> The first visit of the day can take up to a minute while it wakes up. After that it's quick.

## 2. Ask

Type a question the way you'd ask a colleague:

- *Which of my students have an IEP?*
- *Who in my classes gets extended time?*
- *What happens on a second cell phone violation?*
- *Where do students take tests in a separate setting?*

The small numbers in an answer are **citations**. Click one to see the exact passage it came from. If Local School can't find something in the school's documents, it says so instead of guessing.

## 3. Keep it in your Dock (optional)

All the options are on one page: **https://highlightcenter1234--localschool-app-web.modal.run/get**

- **Easiest:** in Safari choose **File → Add to Dock**. Local School then opens like an app.
- **The Mac app:** it adds **⌥ Space** (Option + Space), which opens Local School from anywhere. Open **Terminal**, paste this line and press Return:

  ```
  curl -fsSL https://highlightcenter1234--localschool-app-web.modal.run/install/mac.sh | bash
  ```

  It installs Local School in Applications and opens it, already connected.

  Don't download the app in your browser. macOS will say “Not Opened” for an app downloaded that way. The Terminal line above avoids that.

## Privacy

This pilot link holds only **fictional demo data**. Don't upload real student information here. Real student data belongs on your school's own Local School server, which never sends it off-site.

Questions or problems? Tell your Local School administrator.
