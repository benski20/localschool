# Teacher's Desk: a guide for teachers

Teacher's Desk answers questions about **your school's documents** and **your own class roster**. Every answer shows where it came from.

## 1. Open it

Open this link in Safari or Chrome:

**https://teachers-desk-xi.vercel.app**

- **Just looking?** Press **Try the demo**. You'll be a demo teacher at a fictional school (Maple Grove Middle School).
- **Have an account?** Sign in with the email and password your administrator gave you. To change your password, use the key icon at the bottom left.

> The first visit of the day can take up to a minute while it wakes up. After that it's quick.

## 2. Ask

Type a question the way you'd ask a colleague:

- *Which of my students have an IEP?*
- *Who in my classes gets extended time?*
- *What happens on a second cell phone violation?*
- *Where do students take tests in a separate setting?*

The small numbers in an answer are **citations**. Click one to see the exact passage it came from. If Teacher's Desk can't find something in the school's documents, it says so instead of guessing.

Pick **Fast** or **Thorough** under the question box. Thorough takes a little longer and is better for tricky questions. To ask about your own file (a unit plan, a letter), click the paperclip. Files you attach stay **private to you**.

## 3. Tools

Open **Tools** in the sidebar. Each tool makes a **draft** for you to check and edit. Nothing is sent and nothing is graded.

- **Meeting notes.** Choose the meeting type, confirm that everyone agreed to be recorded, and press **Start recording**. When you stop, you get a summary, decisions and action items, each linked to the moment it was said. Press **Approve** and the recording is deleted. You can also upload a recording or paste a transcript.
- **Feedback.** Paste student work, or drop in a stack of files, and choose a rubric. You get strengths and next steps that quote the student's own words.
- **Adapt a reading.** Paste a text and pick a reading level, a student (their accommodations are applied) or a language.
- **Parent email.** Pick a student, say what it's about in a few words, and choose a tone. Copy it, or open it in your Mail app.

## 4. Keep it in your Dock (optional)

All the options are on one page: **https://teachers-desk-xi.vercel.app/get**

- **Easiest:** in Safari choose **File → Add to Dock**. Teacher's Desk then opens like an app.
- **The Mac app:** it adds **⌥ Space** (Option + Space), which opens Teacher's Desk from anywhere. Open **Terminal**, paste this line and press Return:

  ```
  curl -fsSL https://teachers-desk-xi.vercel.app/install/mac.sh | bash
  ```

  It installs Teacher's Desk in Applications and opens it, already connected.

  Don't download the app in your browser. macOS will say “Not Opened” for an app downloaded that way. The Terminal line above avoids that.

## Privacy

This pilot link holds only **fictional demo data**. Don't upload real student information here. Real student data belongs on your school's own Teacher's Desk server, which never sends it off-site.

Questions or problems? Tell your Teacher's Desk administrator.
