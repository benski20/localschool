Fictional sample for the live eval (attached privately to a question, then deleted).

## The trip

Grade 7 science classes visit the Riverside Science Museum on Friday, November 14.
Buses leave the front circle at 8:15 am and return by 2:30 pm. Students bring a bag lunch.

## Permission forms

Signed permission forms are due Friday, November 7. The trip costs $12 per student;
families can pay online or send cash in an envelope marked "Museum trip".
Chaperones must sign up with Ms. Rivera by November 3.
